The Organisation

Where science shapes the future

The National Physical Laboratory is the UK's national measurement institute — a world-class centre of science, engineering and technology that underpins every aspect of modern life.

From defining the second that powers GPS to developing quantum technologies for a post-GPS world, NPL operates at the frontier of what is possible. The National Timing Centre programme — which this role sits within — is one of the UK's most strategically important resilience programmes, securing the timing infrastructure that financial markets, telecoms networks and critical national infrastructure depend on.

Backed by £180 million of government investment, the programme is building a world-first resilient time distribution capability, giving industry a robust complement to satellite systems such as GPS, which can be disrupted by solar storms, jamming and spoofing. It will support essential services including telecommunications, online banking, emergency response and transport networks.

🇬🇧 National Impact at Scale

NPL operates as a world-leading national laboratory under DSIT. Its research shapes policy, enables industries and protects critical infrastructure across the UK and internationally.

The Opportunity

What you will own

A leadership role at the intersection of cyber security architecture, national infrastructure and government-scale delivery. Reporting to the Head of Cyber Security, you will be the Security Pillar Lead for the National Timing Centre programme.

  • Lead the Cyber Security architecture and design function across NPL's PNT programmes, delivering at national scale.
  • Oversee design, delivery and operationalisation of Cyber capability, developing enterprise architecture aligned with NPL's guiding principles.
  • Lead the technical Cyber Security design of systems and services across multiple PNT programmes at an inter-organisational level.
  • Research, identify, validate and adopt new security technologies, architectures and methodologies.
  • Lead engagement with NPL's customers across UK Government and the private sector on security risk and decision-making.
  • Communicate current and emerging threats, while designing security architecture to mitigate against them.
